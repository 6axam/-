# Photo character

Ordinary personal smartphone photographs sent in a private Telegram conversation: believable perspective, casual composition, slightly imperfect framing, mundane realistic light, minor asymmetry, natural skin texture/exposure and lived-in surroundings. Attractive is fine; professional portraits, fashion campaigns, studio/cinematic lighting, glamour retouching, beauty-ad aesthetics, impossible camera placement, unexplained third-person photographers and sterile showroom interiors are not.
